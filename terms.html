3. Medical fitness and health

3.1 Uru Games is a physically demanding sporting event involving running, resistance exercise, repetitive movements, jumping, ground contact and carrying or moving external loads.

3.2 By registering, the Athlete confirms that they are physically capable of participating in the Event and that they have considered their own health and fitness before entering.

3.3 The Athlete is responsible for obtaining medical advice before participating if they have any medical condition, injury, symptoms, recent illness, or other concern that could affect their ability to participate safely.

3.4 The Athlete must not participate if they know or reasonably believe that participation could place their health or safety, or the safety of others, at unreasonable risk.

3.5 The Athlete must immediately inform an Event Official or medical personnel if they experience significant pain, dizziness, unusual shortness of breath, chest pain, faintness, confusion, severe weakness, injury or any other concerning symptom during the Event.

3.6 The Organiser, Event Officials or medical personnel may stop or withdraw an Athlete from the Event where they reasonably believe that continuing could compromise the Athlete’s health or safety or the safety of others. This will lead to the athlete not completing the Event.

3.7 The Athlete agrees to follow reasonable instructions given by Event Officials, medical personnel and safety personnel.

4. Assumption of inherent sporting risks

4.1 The Athlete acknowledges that participation in Uru Games involves inherent risks associated with strenuous physical activity and competitive sport.

4.2 These risks may include, without limitation:

  • sprains, strains and soft-tissue injuries;

  • aggravation of a pre-existing condition;
  • injury arising from running or uneven surfaces;
  • injury arising from lifting, carrying, swinging or moving weights;

5. Event format and rules

5.1 The Athlete agrees to participate according to the official Uru Games format published by the Organiser.

5.2 The Event currently consists of:

  • an initial 450 m run;
  • Zone 1 — three consecutive MetCon stations;
  • a category-specific run between Zone 1 and Zone 2;
  • Zone 2 — Burpees to Plate;
  • a category-specific run between Zone 2 and Zone 3;
  • Zone 3 — Plate ground to overhead;
  • a short final run to the finish.

5.3 Beginner and Pro categories may have different running distances, repetitions and/or prescribed loads.

5.4 The Organiser may publish or amend detailed Event Rules, course maps, movement standards, prescribed weights, time limits, start procedures and other operational information before the Event.

5.5 The Athlete agrees to comply with the Event Rules and Movement Standards applicable to their category.

5.6 Where a conflict exists between promotional material and the final published Event Rules, the final Event Rules issued by the Organiser shall prevail.

6. Movement standards and judging

6.1 Each Athlete is responsible for understanding the prescribed movement standards before beginning their race.

6.2 Event Officials and Judges will determine whether a repetition meets the prescribed standard.

6.3 A repetition that does not meet the required standard may be declared a NO REP and will not count towards the Athlete’s required repetitions.

6.4 The Athlete must repeat a no-rep where required by the Judge.

6.5 Examples of movements used in Uru Games include:

  • Dumbbell Thrusters
  • Russian Kettlebell Swings
  • Dumbbell Alternating Walking Lunges
  • Burpees to Plate
  • Plate ground to Overhead

6.6 The Organiser may provide briefing material, demonstrations or athlete briefings before the Event. Athletes are responsible for familiarising themselves with the applicable standards and the course.

6.7 The Judge’s decision concerning whether a repetition meets the published movement standard will generally be final for the purpose of competition, subject to any formal protest or appeals process announced by the Organiser.

7. Fair play and competitive integrity

7.1 Uru Games is intended to be a fair and competitive sporting event.

7.2 Athletes must:

  • complete the prescribed course;
  • complete the prescribed repetitions;
  • use only the equipment and loads assigned to their category;
  • follow the prescribed movement standards;
  • follow instructions from Judges and Event Officials;
  • avoid deliberately obstructing another Athlete;
  • and compete honestly.

7.3 The following may result in disqualification:

  • deliberately bypassing part of the course;
  • deliberately performing repetitions incorrectly to gain an advantage;
  • receiving unauthorised physical assistance;
  • using unauthorised equipment or loads;
  • receiving unauthorised pacing or physical assistance;
  • impersonating another Athlete;
  • competing under another person’s registration;
  • tampering with timing or identification systems;
  • aggressive or abusive behaviour towards Officials, volunteers or other Athletes;
  • or any other conduct that gives an unfair competitive advantage.

The Athlete understands these shortcomings of the venue and takes care of their own safety accordingly.
