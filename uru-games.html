URU GAMES is Bangalore's first hybrid fitness race at Agara Lake — a lakeside run into a circuit of functional movement stations, a heavy farmer carry, and a thirty-burpee finish, chip-timed start to finish.

You start by the water. You hit the stations with your heart rate already up. You carry the load, you finish the burpees, and you're done — chip time posted the moment you cross.

01

The playground circuit

01

Lake Run

400 m along Agara Lake to get the legs moving and the pace set.

02

Movement Stations

Wall balls, box step-ups and kettlebell swings — three functional stations, back to back.

03

Farmer Carry

Heavy carry, no shortcuts. Pick up the load and own your space.

04

Burpee Finish

Thirty burpees and you're done — high-fives at the line.

Built for every age group, body type and fitness level. Whether you're eighteen or sixty, a seasoned athlete or taking on your first event — if you can move, you belong here.
