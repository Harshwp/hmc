URU GAMES is Bangalore's first hybrid fitness race at Agara Lake: a single continuous course combining running with two scored workout zones, chip-timed per zone and overall.

You start running. You arrive at a workout zone with your heart rate already up and the clock already running. You finish the work, you run again, and you do it a second time.

01

The course

01

Start

The gun goes and the course opens with a run. You reach the first zone already working.

02

Zone 1 · MetCon

Scored for time. Thrusters, kettlebell swings and walking lunges, back to back.

03

Zone 2 · Endurance

Back onto the course, then burpees to plate. The longest stretch of the race.

04

Finisher Zone

Recovery, hydration and awards. Chip times posted per zone and overall.

Both categories run the same course. They differ only in workout volume and run distance.
