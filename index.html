We're not a gym

We're not
a gym.

We aren't here to lecture you on complex training jargon or preach like fitness gurus. We're a crew of normal, everyday people who figured out that sweating together beats working out alone every single time.

Based in Bangalore, Hybrid Miles Club is where squad energy meets outdoor movement. You don't need to be an elite athlete or a seasoned racer to belong here. We show up three times a week to run, move, encourage each other, and grow a little better together.

Whether you're chasing a personal best or just looking for a solid group to keep you accountable, we've got your back.

Community highlights

The Hardest Mile

27 June 2026 · 75 athletes

Our debut event, and one of the most brutal mile formats in the community: 400 m burpees, 400 m walking lunges, 400 m bear crawl, 400 m run, back to back, no breathing room. It wasn't about being fastest. It was about taking on a crazy challenge and cheering each other through every single lap. Zero dropouts. The kind of morning that gets people talking for weeks.

HMC Gym Crawl

12 July 2026 · 102 sign-ups

Part workout, part neighbourhood crawl, 100% community vibe. We ditched the traditional gym session and took the party on foot across three local fitness hubs. Warmed up at LIFTR, ran to HYFIT for a full station workout, and cooled down at KaizenKlub with breakfast and an ice plunge. 7.56 km, three coached stations, one epic morning.
